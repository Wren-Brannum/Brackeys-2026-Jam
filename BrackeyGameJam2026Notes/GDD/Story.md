In 2062 the country of Alistraza has fallen into a deep depression. With many turning to crime to make ends meet few feel safe joining the police force. In desperation the government has rolled out a new program. A top of the line AI robot from another country will overtake integrations. The AI "Drongo " is tasked with asking suspects about there whereabouts, condition, and relation to materials found at crime scene, then using collected data to determine the truth.
